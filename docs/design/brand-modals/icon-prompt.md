# App icon

Concept: **personal stamp**. An interlocking ivory RC monogram on warm vermilion replaces the cyan gauge. Shawn selected this concept from three generated previews. The broad letterforms remain readable at Home Screen size. Leave generous clear space in other placements; the iOS asset fills its square canvas and relies on the system mask.

Prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Preview concept A for an iOS app named Readiness Coach. Generate a single square app-icon artwork, full bleed flat warm vermilion #D95436 background, centered chunky ivory #F4E9D6 custom RC monogram. Idea: personal stamp. Interlock the uppercase R and C as one memorable bold typographic silhouette; make both letters readable, broad counters and very thick strokes, slightly editorial and athletic but not italic racing typography. Optically balanced, icon occupies 65% of square with generous margin. Strictly flat two color graphic, crisp edges. No circular gauge, arrow, slash, heart, pulse, bars, generic fitness symbol, gradient, shadow, texture, 3D, outer border, rounded tile baked in, caption or watermark. Only RC lettering, no other text. Distinctive personal coaching app, not a stock tech logo.
